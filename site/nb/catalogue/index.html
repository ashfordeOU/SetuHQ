8 376 oppføringer, søkbare.

Alle 8 376 planetene i exoplanet.eu, inkludert de 327 uten lagret avstand som Melkeveikartet ikke kan plassere. SetuHQ har ikke oppdaget disse planetene; det katalogiserer dem.

Known planetsEverything that dims

Data: The Extrasolar Planets Encyclopaedia (exoplanet.eu), fetched 2026-10-18. https://exoplanet.eu/catalog/. The NASA Exoplanet Archive could not be reached when this list was fetched, so it comes from exoplanet.eu alone. exoplanet.eu lists as confirmed every object it treats as a planet: bodies up to 60 Jupiter masses (within their uncertainty), published or announced. Its list is broader than the NASA Exoplanet Archive's, which keeps objects up to 30 Jupiter masses from refereed papers. A value the source does not give reads "not stored".
