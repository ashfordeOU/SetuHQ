8,376 records, searchable.

All 8,376 planets in exoplanet.eu, including the 327 with no stored distance that the Galaxy map cannot place. SetuHQ did not discover these planets; it catalogues them.

Data: The Extrasolar Planets Encyclopaedia (exoplanet.eu), fetched 2026-10-18. https://exoplanet.eu/catalog/. NASA Exoplanet Archive not used: HTTP Error 400: Bad Request. exoplanet.eu lists as confirmed every object it treats as a planet: bodies up to 60 Jupiter masses (within their uncertainty), published or announced. Its list is broader than the NASA Exoplanet Archive's, which keeps objects up to 30 Jupiter masses from refereed papers. A value the source does not give reads "not stored".
