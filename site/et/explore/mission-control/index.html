Keyboard

F6 and Shift+F6 move between panes. Alt+1 to Alt+6 (or Ctrl+Alt+1 to 6, where Alt+number switches browser tabs) jump to one. With a pane focused, Alt+← and Alt+→ move it earlier or later. Drag a pane's title bar onto another to swap them, or use its arrow buttons.
