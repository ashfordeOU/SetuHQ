Transit

When a planet crosses its star, it blocks a little light. The dip's depth gives the planet's size relative to the star; its repetition gives the period. This is what SetuHQ does: its run found pi Men c's transits in TESS sector 1. Open SetuHQ's Transit case ›

What shapes the dip

The depth is roughly (Rp/R★)². The star is brighter at its centre than its edge (limb darkening), so the bottom of the dip is rounded, not flat, and a central crossing dips deeper than k² (Mandel & Agol 2002; Claret 2017 coefficients for TESS). The four contact points set the total duration T14 and the flat-bottom duration T23 (Winn 2010).
