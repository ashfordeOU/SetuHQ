From the search feed: 24 entries from 2 other stars

SetuHQ's search batches run the same panel over more TESS stars. These entries are leads for review: 24 of 24 match a known TOI period, and none has a case file yet. A TOI match is an archive comparison. Archive values only. This page claims no detection.

Feed rule. An entry joins the feed when its depth is at most 5,000 ppm and its signal has at least 2 transits. This is an admission rule, not a vetting result.
