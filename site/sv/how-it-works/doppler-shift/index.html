Mot oss och bort från oss

Light from a star moving toward us arrives slightly bluer; moving away, slightly redder. Only the motion along our line of sight shifts the light. Motion across the sky (proper motion) shifts nothing.

How small the shift is

Δλ/λ = v_r/c, with v_r positive for motion away (redshift). For a wobble of tens of m/s the shift is a ten-millionth of the wavelength, so spectrographs measure it by cross-correlating thousands of lines (Fischer et al. 2016). The star's colour does not visibly change.
