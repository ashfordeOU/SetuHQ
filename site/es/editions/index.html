Why it matters

Anyone can search a TESS light curve, see every check behind a verdict and repeat the numbers. The open core is the whole engine, and everything on this site runs on it.

Institutions need what a public repository cannot give: scale, privacy, packaging and accountability. Pro sells those, never better science: every Pro number would come from the open engine, which anyone can rerun for free.

When the question is yours but the time is not: Ashforde OÜ runs the open engine on your question and hands back a result you can rerun with the free code.

Who it is for
  • Researchers, students and reviewers who need results they can rerun and cite
  • Contributors who run a sweep and send it in
  • Developers who wire SetuHQ into code or an AI assistant over MCP
  • Follow-up and mission teams that keep work private until release
  • AI providers and research groups that want a reviewer measured on the frozen benchmark
  • Universities, planetariums and app makers
  • Teams that need a sector sweep or a candidate dossier
  • Instrument teams that want a yield study
What is included
  • The search, the Sentinels and Probes, vetting and the transit fit
  • The frozen real-TOI benchmark and the public leaderboard
  • The sweep, the MCP server, this site and the stand-alone case files, plates and reports
  • Flares, pulsations and public CHEOPS light curves (CHEOPS data under ESA's terms)
  • Pro Calibrate in development: a calibration certificate for an AI reviewer on the frozen 662-TOI benchmark
  • Pro Workspace in development: private target lists, embargoed candidates, team roles and an audit trail
  • Pro Sweeps in development: hosted searches over sectors or target lists, with the customer's own AI provider key; results come back as open contribution bundles
  • Pro Follow-up in development: a follow-up packet per candidate: an ExoFOP-style export, an ephemeris with its uncertainty, and when the target is observable from the customer's telescopes
  • Education & Dome in development: classroom vetting exercises and a planetarium edition
  • Commercial licence & SDK in development: the engine inside a closed product, with a stable API
  • A sector sweep, a candidate dossier or a yield study for a proposed instrument
  • The open engine as it is: no new code, every figure labelled as on this site
  • Candidates stay candidates: a study is never a confirmation of a planet
Terms

Free under the AGPL-3.0-only, with no feature gate. The same code is also offered under a commercial licence from Ashforde OÜ.

Licensing: one engine, two licences

The same code under either licence; nothing is held back from either.

  • GNU AGPL-3.0-only. Use, study, change and share SetuHQ for free. If you run a changed version as a network service, you offer its users the source.
  • A commercial licence from Ashforde OÜ, for use the AGPL does not suit: SetuHQ inside a closed product, or a hosted service without the source offer. It starts with a message.

SetuHQ's own data, benchmark, leaderboard data and paper are under CC BY 4.0; third-party data keeps its own terms. The plain-language guide: LICENSING.md.

CHEOPS data stay outside Pro and the commercial licence

SetuHQ reads public CHEOPS light curves. Those data, and every feature derived from them, stay under ESA's terms (CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO): they are never part of SetuHQ Pro or of the commercial licence, and any commercial use needs ESA's prior written authorisation (data.licences@esa.int).
