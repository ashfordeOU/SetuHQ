Як малюються зображення.

Кожну зорю в SetuHQ намальовано в кольорі абсолютно чорного тіла за її збереженою температурою. Кожна планета — художнє бачення: її вигляд вибирають за двома виміряними величинами, радіусом (без нього — масою) і світлом, яке вона отримує від своєї зорі. Ніщо на зображенні не виміряне понад ці числа, а склад — ніколи.

Правила

Composition, from the radius in Earth radii (1 RJ = 11.209 R⊕):

Drawn asRadiusWhy
rockbelow 1.6 R⊕Rogers 2015, ApJ 801, 41: most planets larger than about 1.6 R⊕ are not rocky
envelopebelow 4 R⊕the radius valley at 1.5–2.0 R⊕ (Fulton et al. 2017, AJ 154, 109); sub-Neptunes up to 4 R⊕
neptunebelow 10 R⊕the catalogue's size-class boundary (Neptune-like 4–10 R⊕)
giant10 R⊕ or morethe catalogue's size-class boundary (gas giant 10 R⊕ or more)

Without a radius, from the mass (or m sin i, a minimum mass) in Earth masses; such a planet is drawn at a fixed size, marked not to scale. With neither, no planet is drawn.

Drawn asMassWhy
rockbelow 2 M⊕below 2 M⊕
ambiguousbelow 10 M⊕2–10 M⊕: rock or envelope, not decidable from mass
neptunebelow 50 M⊕10–50 M⊕
giant50 M⊕ or more50 M⊕ or more

Temperature band, from the equilibrium temperature Teq = 278.3 K × S1/4, where S = R★² (Teff / 5,772 K)⁴ / a² is the light the planet gets relative to Earth (zero albedo, full redistribution). Without a, a comes from Kepler's third law; without R★ or Teff, the catalogue's stored Teq is used; without that, the colours are neutral.

BandTeqWhy
coldbelow 200 Kwater ice is stable at the surface
temperatebelow 350 Kspans the conservative habitable-zone fluxes; named temperate, not habitable
warmbelow 1,000 Kbetween temperate and hot
hotbelow 2,000 Kthe substellar point reaches √2·Teq ≥ 1,414 K, about the basalt solidus; hot Jupiters are conventionally ≥ 1,000 K
ultrahot2,000 K or moreultra-hot Jupiters (Parmentier et al. 2018, A&A 617, A110)

A star's colour: a Planck spectrum from 380 to 780 nm, through the CIE 1931 colour-matching functions (the multi-lobe fit of Wyman, Sloan and Shirley 2013), to sRGB, normalised so the colour is a hue, not a brightness. Limb darkening is the quadratic law with Claret's (2017) TESS-band coefficients, applied to a visible disc. Granulation and planet surfaces are texture only: decorative, never data.

The 18 looks

Each look as its still (seed 1). Oceans and continents are drawn only for Earth itself, the Solar System reference; no exoplanet is given water.

Колір зорі

The colour is computed, not picked: Planck's law at the star's Teff, sampled from 380 to 780 nm in 5 nm steps, weighted by the CIE 1931 colour-matching functions (the multi-lobe fit of Wyman, Sloan and Shirley 2013), converted from XYZ to linear sRGB, normalised to the brightest channel and gamma-encoded. Teff is clamped to 1,000–40,000 K. Because of the normalisation every star is equally bright on screen: the colour is a hue, not a luminosity. The limb darkening is the quadratic law with Claret's (2017) coefficients for the TESS band, a red-optical law applied to a visible disc.
