What this page does not claim

  • No confirmed planet. Every dip, signal and feed entry here is a candidate, unconfirmed. Passing vetting is not a confirmation. The match with pi Men c says the planet is already known, not that this run confirmed it.
  • No planet sizes. No stellar radius is stored, so a depth gives only a planet-to-star radius ratio. The Planets page shows the working.
  • No people on the panel. The panel is the Sentinels and the Probes: 2 Sentinels and 3 Probes. Sentinels run on Claude (Anthropic). Probes are deterministic matched-filter detectors. Their weights rest on calibration with 10 planted and 10 empty light curves each, so they are rough.
  • Archive values only. This page claims no detection. A TOI or archive match compares a stored period with published values.
  • No image of the planet. The planet on the Overview is an artist's impression; the field stars on the sky map are decorative.
