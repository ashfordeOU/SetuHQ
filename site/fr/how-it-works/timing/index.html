Chronométrage et phase

A transit is one moment in an orbit. The rest of the orbit carries information too: when transits arrive, how the planet glows as it turns, when it slips behind its star, and which half of the spinning star it covers.

SetuHQ measures each dip's time and checks the odd and even dips against each other; it does not fit timing variations, phase curves or eclipses. The secondary-eclipse depth on the canvas is drawn ten times deeper and says so; the TTV amplitude is illustrative. Spin–orbit angles span aligned, polar and retrograde orbits (Albrecht et al. 2022).
