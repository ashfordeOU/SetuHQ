Where TESS is looking.

The NASA TESS satellite watches one strip of sky for about four weeks (a sector), with four cameras of 24° by 24° each. This map shows the strips from the published schedule, and the stars with known planets inside them.

Pointing from the published schedule, not live telemetry. Schedule saved on 2026-10-24; sectors 104 to 121. The satellite can change plans; the TESS Science Office's pages are the authority.

SetuHQ's own runs use TESS data already public at MAST; this page only shows where the satellite is scheduled to look. The sky map ›
