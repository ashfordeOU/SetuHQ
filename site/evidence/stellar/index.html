Stars, measured.

Beyond planets, SetuHQ detects stellar flares, extracts pulsation frequencies and reads public CHEOPS light curves. Each capability was compared with published results under a protocol committed before its run. None is fully validated, and each page and tool output says how far it is.

  • Flares

    partly validated

  • Pulsations

    partly validated

    7 of 9 counted checks pass

  • CHEOPS light curves

    not validated

    2 of 5 counted checks pass; 2 comparisons inconclusive (gated)

Protocol v3 (docs/results/stellar_validation_protocol_v3.json, sha256 c978f04c24b8…), frozen 2026-10-22, committed in b2ac54c26149 before its run. Rule: validated when every counted check passes; partly validated when more than half do; otherwise not validated. Protocol v3 re-tests the targets earlier versions used, so it is a re-test after review fixes, not an independent validation.

Not validated

  • Flare energies: on TESS they use the TESS magnitude and distance (Sullivan et al. 2015 zero point) when the TIC has them, else TIC Teff and radius; on CHEOPS (no magnitude route) the TIC radius and, where the TIC has no Teff, the Teff from the CHEOPS archive, labelled. A star with neither route gets energies reported as unavailable.
  • AU Mic sector 1 is the tuning star of the flare shape test: its numbers are reported, its null is not counted.
  • GJ 1243: no machine-readable per-flare TESS catalogue was found; detections are reported, not compared.
  • GJ 65: no per-flare table is public for Bruno et al. (2024); only an internal 120 s vs 20 s check.
  • Poyatos et al. (2025) worked on 3-s CHEOPS imagettes reduced with PIPE; DACE serves DRP subarray light curves (about 42 s and longer), so the CHEOPS comparison is of aggregate numbers on different data products, not flare by flare, and it counts only when the CHEOPS null and injection gates pass. Their per-flare catalogue is not public.
  • Bruno et al. (2024): no per-flare catalogue; their complex fraction (≳ 30%) is quoted for context only.
  • Wavelet denoising: tuned and checked by injection on SetuHQ's own data; Poyatos et al.'s optimal parameters were found on imagettes and are not reproduced here.
  • HR 10 CHEOPS frequencies: Salmon et al. (2024) publish no CHEOPS frequency list; only the TESS lists are compared.

CHEOPS: not validated (2 of 5 checks pass)

SetuHQ reads public CHEOPS light curves (the Data Reduction Pipeline's photometry) through DACE, the University of Geneva's public API in front of the CHEOPS Mission Archive; ESA does not designate DACE. Only visits the archive marks as public are read, with no credentials, and no raw CHEOPS file is stored in this repository or shipped in the package. The CHEOPS capability is not validated: its null and injection gates failed, so two comparisons are inconclusive, and the end-to-end match with pycheops' product failed. The CHEOPS-derived numbers are not on this page: they are in stellar_validation_cheops.md, under ESA's terms.
