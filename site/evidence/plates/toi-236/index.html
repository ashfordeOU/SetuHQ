SetuHQ plates: TOI-236

6 dips (6 rejected) on 1 star, one plate per star, from run tess-toi-236-s2. Each dip that passed vetting is a candidate, unconfirmed, and is tagged so; a rejected or flagged dip is named by its status alone. Every number is a stored value.

  • BLS periods and epochs are found per 13-day subject (a fixed-length cut of a sector), not per sector.
  • The panel is the Sentinels and the Probes, no people. Sentinels run on Claude (Anthropic) by default; other providers are supported and labelled. Probes are deterministic matched-filter detectors. A dip's consensus interval is where their marks agree. Probes only; Sentinels not run on this example.
  • No stellar radius is stored, so no depth on this page is converted to a planet radius.
  • Status is written as a word beside every colour.

River of time the whole feed

One row per star, a tick per candidate. Height is log SNR; colour is status, and each row lists its statuses in words. Stretches of more than 30 days with no candidate are cut, so the segments are not drawn to a common time scale: each has its own, labelled at its start and end, with its span in days (d) beneath it. A known TOI period is an archive value: Archive values only. This page claims no detection.
