## What you may do without asking

- **Say what you use.** "Uses SetuHQ", "built on SetuHQ", "a fork of SetuHQ", "scored on the
  SetuHQ real-TOI leaderboard", in text, to describe accurately what your work does.
- **Cite it.** Name SetuHQ in papers, talks, teaching material and articles, with the citation
  in [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff).
- **Link to it** with the name or with the unmodified logo, following the usage rules in
  [`docs/assets/brand/README.md`](docs/assets/brand/README.md).
- **Run it unchanged.** Run an unmodified copy of this repository, built from a tagged release or a
  commit of `ashfordeOU/SetuHQ`, and call it SetuHQ.

## What needs a different name

- **Forks and derivative distributions must rename.** If you distribute a modified version, or
  offer one as a service, give it a name and a mark that are not "SetuHQ", "Setu" or confusingly
  similar to them, and remove the Setu mark and the SetuHQ logo from its pages, package metadata
  and icons. You may say it is "based on SetuHQ".
- **No implied endorsement.** Do not suggest that Ashforde OÜ made, reviewed, endorses or supports
  your product, service, results or candidates unless it has agreed to in writing.
- **No SetuHQ-branded results from changed code.** Do not label results, catalogues, scores or
  leaderboard entries "SetuHQ" when they come from modified code or rules; name your fork instead.
- **Package and domain names.** Do not publish a package, plugin, app, MCP server, domain or
  account under "setuhq", "setu" or a confusingly similar name, except by Ashforde OÜ.
